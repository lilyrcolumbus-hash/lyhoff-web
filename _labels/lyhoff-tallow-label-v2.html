Same wrap size as before (8.5" × 1.5"). The front / Principal Display Panel is now block 1 (not centered), at normal PDP width. Ingredients, directions and manufacturer stay off the front. Monogram = the open-top "LF" frame (open above = "nothing to hide") — shown on some variants as a packaging seal, per brand rules it never replaces the wordmark.

Taglines — brand + per-product

The italic line I used, "Skin that actually heals.", is the catalogue tagline. Proposal: keep a short brand line, plus a product-specific line for each item. For Beef Tallow the natural territory is the "nothing to hide" idea (two ingredients, readable).
